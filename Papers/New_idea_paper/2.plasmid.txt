In typing.csv, mash_neighbor_identification is the reported bacterial identification of the closest matching reference plasmid found by MOB-typer’s Mash search. It describes the neighbor, not necessarily the organism from which your plasmid was collected.

Read the three fields together:

mash_nearest_neighbor: the matching reference plasmid
mash_neighbor_distance: how different its sequence is from your plasmid; smaller means more similar
mash_neighbor_identification: the organism associated with that reference plasmid

For your plasmid’s own recorded organism, use TAXONOMY_species after joining taxonomy.csv. If the two organisms differ, that may be interesting to investigate, but this field alone does not demonstrate plasmid transfer between them. The PLSDB README (plasmid_study/README.md) defines it as the host taxonomy of the database match.

## plasfinder results
organism_L1 and organism_L2 in plasmid_study/plasmidfinder.csv.

They label the organism group of the PlasmidFinder reference database used for that replicon hit. L1 is the broad group; L2 is its more specific database category. In this file, the L1 groups are “Enterobacteriales” and “Gram positive.” These are database labels, not formal taxonomic ranks.

They do not identify the organism that carried your PLSDB plasmid. For your ESKAPE selection, use TAXONOMY_genus and TAXONOMY_species from taxonomy.csv; use organism_L1/L2 only to interpret the PlasmidFinder hit.

##accession column is the GenBank accession of the reference plasmid in the PlasmidFinder database associated with that hit, as described in the README (plasmid_study/README.md).
